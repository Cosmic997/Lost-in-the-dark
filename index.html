The Lore

What Lurks Within

The Forsaken Manor was once home to a wealthy family who dabbled in dark rituals. One night, their ceremonies tore a rift between worlds — and something crossed over.

The entity bound itself to three cursed relics, anchoring itself to the manor. Whoever possesses the relics can weaken the entity — but only those who find the exit key can truly leave.

Many have entered. None have returned. The manor's halls shift in the dark. The walls whisper. And it is always watching.

Now you wake on the cold stone floor, flashlight flickering, with no memory of how you arrived. The door behind you has vanished. The only way out... is through.

Gameplay

Survive the Night

Every mechanic is designed to keep you on edge. The darkness is your enemy — and your only ally.

🔦
Flashlight System
Aim your flashlight with the mouse. Battery drains over time. Turn it off to hide — but the darkness eats your sanity.
👾
Entity AI
The entity patrols, hunts, and searches. It sees your flashlight from far away. In the dark, you're invisible — but not safe.
🪔
Cursed Relics
Collect 3 relics to weaken the entity. Each one makes it slower and less perceptive — but also angrier.
🧠
Sanity Mechanic
Stay in darkness too long and your mind fractures. Hallucinations. Screen distortion. You start seeing things that aren't there.
💀
Jump Scares
Random terrifying events. Faces in the dark. Sudden screams. Not recommended for the faint of heart.
📜
Scattered Notes
Find notes from those who came before. Their warnings. Their madness. Their final words carved into the walls.

Survival Guide

Don't Die

These tips might keep you alive. Probably not though.

  • Turn off your flashlight when you hear the entity nearby. It can't see you in the dark from far away — but staying in darkness drains your sanity.
  • Collect batteries whenever you find them. A dead flashlight means total darkness and rapid sanity loss.
  • Grab all 3 relics as soon as possible. Each relic weakens the entity, making it slower and less able to detect you.
  • Read the notes — they contain lore and subtle hints about where to find key items.
  • The exit key is in the basement. Get it, then find the exit door. You need the key to escape.
  • Don't run in a straight line from the entity. Use walls and corridors to break line of sight.
